🃏 PokerB — the AI is learning right now (live)

Training step — Playing strength — computes instead of guessing — Garbage rate — Pace — updated —

📈 How well does the AI play? ?

Average profit in big blinds per decision (in simulation against the opponent league). Should rise slightly or stay stable-positive — NOT crash toward −10 (that would be a collapse). The grey band is the spread.

🧮 Does it compute instead of guessing? ?

Share of decisions the AI computes with the engine (calls a formula) instead of guessing from memory. Should RISE — that is exactly our goal: compute instead of hallucinate.

🗑️ How often does it produce garbage? ?

Share of invalid/illegal programs (the engine rejects them). Should stay low — below the green 10% line. If it climbs toward the red 20% line, the AI is forgetting the correct format.

⏱️ Pace (sec per step) ?

Seconds per training step. Shows whether we stay within the time budget. High = the CPU evaluation (simulating the hands) is the bottleneck — then the expensive GPU often sits idle.

Exploration ?

How "widely" the AI still explores (entropy). Should NOT drop to 0 — otherwise it stubbornly repeats the same thing and stops learning anything new.

Program length ?

Average length of the generated programs (tokens). Should stay stable — if it runs away, the AI is writing nonsense.

"Nothing to learn" rate ?

Share of spots where all attempts were scored identically = no learning signal there. 0.1–0.5 is healthy; much higher = the spots are too trivial.
